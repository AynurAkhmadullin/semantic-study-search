This project selected prose excerpts from eight sections and removed formatting,
MDX components, images, videos and code examples. It does not reproduce the
entire course. These are adaptations of course text, not original tutorials
written by the author of Semantic Study Search. Original wording is otherwise
retained, including any historical claims and translation imperfections.

corpus.json records each source file, its SHA-256, selected line ranges and URL.
To reconstruct the source URL at this revision, append source_file to:
https://github.com/huggingface/course/blob/8b893f0ede6c781045692a7f892f25a0c92b4805/
Rebuild the corpus with scripts/prepare_demo_corpus.py.
